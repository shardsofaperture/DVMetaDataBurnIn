## Source materials

Corresponding-source/build materials and the complete dependency notice set for
the historical Evermeet FFmpeg binary remain unavailable. The source bundle on
[maintenance preview 2](https://github.com/shardsofaperture/DVMetaDataBurnIn/releases/tag/v1.0.7-maintenance.2)
corresponds to that preview's different FFmpeg build, not the stable binary.
The DVRescue notice supplement accompanies the unchanged historical downloads.
