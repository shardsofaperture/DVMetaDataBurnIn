# 1.0.7 maintenance preview 2

This is an **experimental Intel macOS preview**, not a promotion of 1.0.7 to
stable. [Stable 1.0.6 remains on main](https://github.com/shardsofaperture/DVMetaDataBurnIn/tree/main).
No branch merge or historical asset replacement is part of this release.

[Download preview 2 and its source materials](https://github.com/shardsofaperture/DVMetaDataBurnIn/releases/tag/v1.0.7-maintenance.2).
`BUILD-MANIFEST.json` identifies the exact source commit, application/tool/font
hashes and the accompanying `dependency-sources.tar`. `SHA256SUMS.txt` covers the
published files. The application remains MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 Zach Zarzycki.

- FFmpeg 8.0.1-dvmetadata1 is built from pinned source, statically linking x264,
  FreeType and HarfBuzz with GPLv3-or-later selected. The complete required source
  inputs, original licenses and build script are supplied beside the app.
- DVRescue 24.07-dvmetadata1 uses MediaInfoLib 24.06 and ZenLib 0.4.41 with
  DeckLink/capture/control and simulation disabled. A disclosed source patch
  repairs upstream conditional compilation. No capture SDK is used.
- VT323, Share Tech Mono and IBM Plex Mono replace the three historical fonts.
  Unmodified files and full SIL OFL 1.1 texts are bundled. MKVs attach both the
  selected bundled font and its license; ASS uses its actual family name.
- Output safety and H.264 field-signaling fixes implemented after preview 1 are
  included. Complete decode validation precedes exclusive final publication.
  Existing outputs are never overwritten. APFS/HFS+ destinations are supported;
  filesystems without hard links fail safely.

Validation: 35 synthetic regressions, three shell smoke checks, 11 release-gate
tests and the native Intel Xcode 15.4 Release build. Fresh checks on the original
238-frame train capture preserve its hash, DV/PCM in MOV, 238 BFF H.264 frames
in MP4/MKV, 476 progressive 60p frames, and nine subtitle events through 7.95 s.
The replacement DVRescue produces the same 238 recording-date rows as the old
binary; XML differs only in the modified version string. See [machine-readable
build and test evidence](../audit/2026-11-13/dependency-builds/) and the
[rebuild instructions](../../scripts/dependencies/README.md).

The source delivery preserves all CLI source/license files, with a reproducible
selection script excluding unrelated GUI test videos and prebuilt Windows
libraries from the publisher's large source bundle. It does not redistribute
or replace the existing train video assets.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized, and declares macOS 12 minimum. Tested
build/runtime evidence is Intel macOS 14. GUI queues/Stop, player rendering and
motion/audio acceptance, other cameras, Apple Silicon and Rosetta remain
unverified. Direct DV/Digital8 capture and HDV support remain future goals.

Historical releases, including stable 1.0.6 and preview 1, retain their original
bytes. This source delivery does not correspond to their old Evermeet binary,
and new OFL terms do not license their old UAV/VCR fonts. Historical #194–196
remain open for the exact supplier source, old SDK/build mapping and old-font
permission evidence described in [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md). Those questions
are separate from acceptance of the experimental application implementation.
